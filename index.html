                About
                
        
                
          Everything stays on your phone (localStorage). No accounts, no cloud.

                
          How it works:
                
          1. Add housing, bills (with due days), debts, and goals.
                
          2. Enter a paycheck and generate the split plan.
                
          3. Goals by priority ($/mo each), then leftover % to debt.
                
          4. Mark bills paid with ✅ as you pay them.
                
          5. Weekly Audit + share report with your wife.
                
          6. Export calendar for real iPhone alerts.
